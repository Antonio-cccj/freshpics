Demo images for testing

All content below is fictional. Dates on the boarding pass, coupon and movie ticket are already in the past.

  1. Open this page in Safari on the iPhone and tap an image to view it full screen.
  2. Take a screenshot (side button + volume up) of the boarding pass, coupon and movie ticket. Also take one of the passport to see that ID documents are never recommended.
  3. Open PhotoSweep and tap Scan Again. The screenshots appear under Expired Info, each with a reason.

Pickup codes, verification codes, delivery orders and payment pages are recognized too, but they are only considered expired a few days after the screenshot is taken.
