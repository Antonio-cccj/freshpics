PhotoSweep 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 1 日

PhotoSweep（以下简称“本应用”）是一款在 iPhone 本机整理相册的工具。我们非常重视你的隐私，本政策说明本应用如何处理你的信息。

一句话总结

本应用不收集、不上传、不分享你的任何个人数据。所有照片分析都在你的设备上完成。

1. 相册访问

  • 本应用需要你授权访问“照片”，用于在本机分析照片，找出可能已过期的截图（如取件码、车票、优惠券）、旧截图、相似照片和低质量照片。
  • 你可以选择“允许访问所有照片”或“限制访问”，也可以随时在系统“设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 照片”中更改或撤销授权。
  • 照片内容和分析过程都不会离开你的设备。

2. 本机分析方式

本应用使用 Apple 系统自带的 Vision 框架在设备上完成文字识别、画质评估、人脸检测和相似度计算，不调用任何云端服务或第三方 AI 接口。

3. 本机存储的数据

  • 分析结果（照片的系统标识符、识别出的类别和理由、评分）保存在本应用的沙盒目录中，用于下次增量扫描，不包含照片本身，也不包含识别出的文字内容。
  • 你的偏好设置（如“旧截图”的天数阈值）保存在本机。
  • 删除本应用后，以上数据会被系统一并删除。

4. 删除照片

本应用只给出建议，不会自动删除任何照片。每次删除都需要你在系统弹窗中确认，删除的照片会进入“最近删除”，30 天内可在“照片”应用中恢复。

5. 网络与第三方

  • 本应用不包含广告、统计分析、崩溃上报或任何第三方 SDK，不使用追踪技术。
  • 如果你开启了 iCloud 照片，显示缩略图时系统可能从 iCloud 下载照片的预览图，这一过程由 Apple 处理，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。

6. 儿童隐私

本应用不收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁（或你所在地区规定年龄）以下的儿童。

7. 政策更新

如本政策有变更，我们会更新本页并修改生效日期。若变更涉及数据收集方式，会在应用更新说明中提示。

8. 联系我们

如有任何问题，请发送邮件至 antonio.chu@outlook.com。

PhotoSweep Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

PhotoSweep (“the app”) helps you tidy up the photo library on your iPhone. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

In short

The app does not collect, upload or share any of your personal data. All photo analysis happens on your device.

1. Photo library access

  • The app asks for access to Photos so it can analyze your photos on device and find expired screenshots (such as pickup codes, tickets and coupons), old screenshots, similar photos and low-quality shots.
  • You can grant full or limited access, and change or revoke it at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.
  • Your photos and the analysis never leave your device.

2. How analysis works

The app uses Apple’s built-in Vision framework on your device for text recognition, image quality assessment, face detection and similarity. It does not call any cloud service or third-party AI API.

3. Data stored on your device

  • Analysis results (system photo identifiers, detected categories and reasons, scores) are stored in the app’s sandbox to speed up the next scan. They do not include the photos themselves or any recognized text.
  • Your preferences (for example the “old screenshot” threshold) are stored on your device.
  • Deleting the app removes all of this data.

4. Deleting photos

The app only makes suggestions and never deletes anything automatically. Every deletion requires your confirmation in a system dialog. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted and can be recovered in the Photos app for 30 days.

5. Network and third parties

  • The app contains no ads, analytics, crash reporting or third-party SDKs, and does not track you.
  • If you use iCloud Photos, the system may download photo previews from iCloud to show thumbnails. This is handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

6. Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region).

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Changes to data practices will be noted in the app’s release notes.
