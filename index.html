常见问题

照片会上传吗？

不会。所有分析都在你的 iPhone 上完成，本应用没有服务器，也不联网上传任何数据。

会自动删除照片吗？

不会。本应用只给出建议和理由，删除前需要你在系统弹窗中确认。删除的照片会进入“最近删除”，30 天内可恢复。

为什么有的照片没被识别？

为了避免误删，本应用只推荐把握较大的照片。收藏、加入相簿、编辑过或包含人脸的照片会被降低推荐优先级，身份证、合同、发票等证件类图片永远不会被推荐。

只授权了部分照片怎么办？

本应用只能分析你授权的照片。可在“设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 照片 > PhotoSweep”中改为“完全访问”。

如何恢复误删的照片？

打开“照片”应用 > 相簿 > 最近删除，选择照片后点“恢复”。

FAQ

Are my photos uploaded?

No. All analysis happens on your iPhone. The app has no server and uploads nothing.

Does it delete photos automatically?

No. The app only suggests photos with a reason for each. You confirm every deletion in a system dialog, and deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Why wasn’t a photo detected?

To avoid mistakes, the app only recommends photos it is confident about. Favorites, photos in albums, edited photos and photos with faces are deprioritized, and ID cards, contracts and invoices are never recommended.

I only allowed access to some photos.

The app can only analyze photos you allow. Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > PhotoSweep and choose Full Access.

How do I recover a deleted photo?

Open Photos > Albums > Recently Deleted, select the photo and tap Recover.
